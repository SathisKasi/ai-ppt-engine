### Slide 2: Technical Architecture: The 9-Stage Autonomous Engine

- **Slide Number**: 02
- **Layout Pattern**: `PROCESS_PIPELINE` (3 Core Phases across the 9 Stages)
- **Header Title**: Technical Workflow: From Raw Text to Boardroom Output
- **Executive Takeaway**: A unidirectional, nine-stage autonomous pipeline with rolling context memory and mathematical space budgeting.

#### Pipeline Stages

##### Phase 1: Semantic Ingestion & Map-Reduce Analysis
- **Step Identifier**: `PHASE 01`
- **Focus**: `Stages 1 to 4`
- **Description**: Ingests multi-format documents, applies encoding hygiene, and executes dual-strategy chunking (heading trees or AI sliding windows). Analyzes chunks sequentially with rolling context memory and consolidates themes via Map-Reduce synthesis.
- **Key Artifact**: Validated `ContentAnalysis` JSON (KPIs, themes, timelines, outline)

##### Phase 2: Template-Aware Planning & Content Enrichment
- **Step Identifier**: `PHASE 02`
- **Focus**: `Stages 5 to 6`
- **Description**: The Layout Architect LLM evaluates the cognitive shape of information against the 16:9 canvas. Enriches raw facts into 1-sentence executive takeaways and structured metrics, then compiles a Declarative Component Grid DSL.
- **Key Artifact**: Self-healing `DynamicPresentationPlan` JSON

##### Phase 3: OpenXML Deep-Cloning & Mathematical Rendering
- **Step Identifier**: `PHASE 03`
- **Focus**: `Stages 7 to 9`
- **Description**: Deep-clones Slide 1 (Cover Page), duplicates Slide 2 (Content Canvas) $N$ times while preserving logos and corner accents, computes exact EMU coordinates for cards/tables, appends the official Thank You slide, and delivers an in-memory binary stream.
- **Key Artifact**: Boardroom-ready `.pptx` file (Zero disk artifacts)

- **Speaker Notes**:
  > "Walk the audience through the three phases. Note how Phase 1 eliminates context loss, Phase 2 enriches the content into executive takeaways rather than raw bullets, and Phase 3 uses low-level OpenXML mutation to maintain 100% brand fidelity."
