## 1. Executive Overview & Specifications

The **HLD QBR Template** (`HLD QBR Template PFv3.potx`) is an enterprise-grade presentation system designed for **UPS Healthcare Logistics & Distribution Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)** and Monthly Business Reviews (MBRs). 

Unlike unstructured presentation decks, the HLD QBR template is **archetype-driven**: every business domain (operational metrics, strategic pillars, continuous improvement, compliance, and timelines) has a dedicated, brand-approved layout with pre-calibrated typography, coordinate geometry, and OpenXML object relationships.

### Technical Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen (`12,192,000` × `6,858,000` EMUs / `13.333"` × `7.500"`).
- **Slide Master & Layouts**: 1 Master with 19 specialized layouts (`Cover Blue`, `Title Only`, `1_Section Header_No Image`, `2_Section Header_No Image_Blue BG`, `9_Section Header`, `Blank`, `Title and Content`, etc.).
- **Total Template Slides**: 60 slides structured into:
  - **Core Slides (00–30)**: Standard QBR presentation flow (Executive Summary, Priorities, Dashboards, Charts, Trackers, Next Steps).
  - **Optional / Supplemental Slides (31–40)**: Advanced financial charts, scorecards, and multi-year roadmaps.
  - **Formatting Help & Design System (41–59)**: Official brand guardrails, color palettes, typography rules, sample tables/charts, and an extensive vector icon library.
- **Engine Rules**: The presentation builder automatically strips authoring guidance stickers (e.g. `"Required content"`, `"Example text only"`, `"Formulas in Notes"`), decouples OpenXML chart parts, prunes unused template slides, and guarantees clean outputs.

## 6. Engine OpenXML Automation & Safety Rules

When generating presentations from arbitrary incoming documents, the engine enforces these architectural rules:

1. **Decoupled OpenXML Parts**: Chart slides clone independent `ChartPart` XML (`/ppt/charts/chart{N}.xml`) and independent Excel workbooks (`/ppt/embeddings/Microsoft_Excel_Sheet{N}.xlsx`). No two slides ever share a chart part.
2. **Deterministic Guidance Stripping**: All shapes matching `GUIDANCE_REGEX` (e.g. `"required content"`, `"example"`, `"formulas in notes"`) are purged automatically.
3. **Stray Highlight Removal**: All `<a:highlight>` tags (such as the template's magenta `[CUSTOMER NAME]` tags) are stripped slide-wide.
4. **Mandatory vs. Content Slides**:
   - **4 Mandatory Slides**: Cover (Slide 00), Agenda (Slide 01), Executive Summary (Slide 03), and Closing (Slide 30) are always rendered.
   - **Content Slides**: Populated strictly based on factual evidence in the input document. Unsupported archetypes are omitted with zero empty or broken slides.
5. **Token Budget Compliance**: LLM planning prompts stay strictly under 1,000 tokens (well below the 8,000 token limit).
